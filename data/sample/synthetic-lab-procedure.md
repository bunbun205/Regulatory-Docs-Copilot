# Synthetic Sample Handling Procedure

> SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE. This is not a real procedure, regulation, or agency document.

## 1. Scope

This synthetic procedure describes an invented workflow for handling samples in a
fictional laboratory. It contains no regulatory requirements and must never be
quoted as evidence about a real laboratory.

## 2. Sample Receipt

Samples arrive in sealed containers. A fictional technician records an identifier,
the receipt date, and the storage location in the synthetic sample log. Damaged
containers are placed in a quarantine cabinet and reported to the fictional
laboratory supervisor.

## 3. Storage

Samples are stored in the location named by the synthetic label. The fictional
storage area is checked at the start of each shift for leaks, unusual odors, and
unlabeled containers. Storage temperatures are recorded on the fictional daily
log sheet.

## 4. Disposal

At the end of the synthetic workflow, samples are transferred to the fictional
waste desk. The technician records the transfer and confirms that the container
is closed. Disposal instructions for this fixture are intentionally invented and
have no legal or safety meaning.

## 5. Training

Technicians complete a fictional orientation before working with the synthetic
samples. Refresher training is recorded in the synthetic training notes. The
fixture does not specify a real training interval.
